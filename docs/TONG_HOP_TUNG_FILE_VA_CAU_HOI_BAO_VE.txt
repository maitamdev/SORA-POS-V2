===============================================================================
1. TONG QUAN HE THONG
===============================================================================

Sora POS V2 la he thong quan ly ban hang tai quay cho cua hang ban le.
He thong gom 4 phan lon:

1. Frontend web:
   React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind.
   Dung cho POS, dashboard, san pham, kho, hoa don, ca lam, bao cao, AI.

2. Backend API:
   Express + TypeScript.
   Chiu trach nhiem auth, RBAC, validate, service nghiep vu, goi Supabase.

3. Database:
   PostgreSQL tren Supabase.
   Cac luong quan trong nhu checkout, huy don, nhap kho nam trong SQL RPC de dam bao transaction.

4. Sora Scanner:
   Expo/React Native mobile app.
   Quet barcode, ghep doi POS qua Supabase Realtime, co offline queue khi mat mang.

Diem noi bat khi bao ve:
- Checkout transaction, tranh lech hoa don/ton kho/thanh toan.
- RBAC admin/manager/cashier.
- Audit log cho hanh dong quan trong.
- Bao cao doanh thu, gia von, loi nhuan.
- AI phan tich doanh thu va goi y nhap hang.
- POS offline bang IndexedDB.
- Scanner mobile co offline queue + ACK.
- PayOS/VietQR cho thanh toan chuyen khoan.

3. DATABASE FILES
===============================================================================

database/README.md
- Huong dan thu tu chay migration SQL.
- Quan trong khi deploy Supabase tu dau.

database/schema.sql
- Tao schema goc: users, roles, products, categories, suppliers, customers,
  orders, order_details, payments, stock_transactions, stock_alerts,
  shift_sessions, goods_receipts, ai tables, audit_logs, payment_intents.
- Tao index, foreign key, trigger updated_at.
- Khi bao ve: day la nen tang CSDL quan he cua he thong.

database/seed.sql
- Du lieu mau ban dau: role, user demo, category/product mau neu co.
- Dung de demo nhanh.

database/enterprise_pos_core.sql
- File SQL nghiep vu quan trong nhat.
- Chua RPC create_pos_order, cancel_pos_order, create_goods_receipt, write_audit_log.
- Dam bao checkout trong transaction: kiem tra ca lam, khoa san pham, tru ton, tao order, payment, stock transaction, audit.
- Cau hoi hay bi hoi: "Vi sao dua checkout vao SQL function?"
  Tra loi: de tat ca thao tac thanh cong cung nhau hoac rollback cung nhau, tranh tao don ma chua tru kho.

database/stock_atomic_rpc.sql
- RPC thao tac kho an toan.
- Dung cho import/adjust stock, tranh race condition.

database/expiry_setup.sql
- Bo sung nghiep vu lo hang/han su dung va canh bao het han.
- Co the trinh bay la module mo rong cho cua hang co san pham han dung.

database/app_settings.sql
- Tao bang app_settings.
- Luu cau hinh van hanh: cho ban qua ton khong, muc giam gia toi da, ngan hang VietQR.

database/ai_revenue_analyses.sql
- Tao bang luu lich su AI phan tich doanh thu.
- Luu analysis JSONB, metrics snapshot, period, days, health_score.
- Khi hoi AI co luu CSDL khong: co, trong bang nay.

database/payment_intents.sql
- Tao bang payment_intents cho PayOS.
- Luu order_code, amount, status, checkout_url, qr_code, webhook payload.
- Y nghia: doi soat thanh toan, biet giao dich nao pending/paid/failed.

database/order_details_cost_snapshot.sql
- Them/backfill cost_price cho order_details.
- Y nghia: bao cao loi nhuan qua khu khong bi sai khi gia von san pham hien tai thay doi.

database/hardening.sql
- Bo sung constraint va enable RLS.
- Tang an toan du lieu, chan status/provider/payment method sai.

20.1 Dang nhap va phan quyen
- LoginPage -> auth.store -> auth.api -> auth.routes -> auth.controller -> auth.service.
- Backend verify password bcrypt, tao JWT.
- Moi API sau do qua authMiddleware va roleMiddleware.

20.2 Tao hoa don POS
- POSPage -> usePOSCart/usePOSCheckout -> order.api.create.
- Backend order.routes -> order.controller -> order.service.
- Database create_pos_order:
  1. Kiem tra user/role/shift.
  2. Kiem tra idempotency client_order_number.
  3. Lock product rows FOR UPDATE.
  4. Kiem tra ton kho.
  5. Tao order/order_details/payment.
  6. Tru kho.
  7. Ghi stock_transactions/audit.
  8. Cap nhat diem khach hang.

20.3 Huy hoa don
- OrdersPage -> order.api.cancel -> cancel_pos_order.
- Khong xoa data, chuyen status cancelled/refunded, co the restock, ghi audit.

20.4 Nhap kho
- CreateReceiptPage -> goodsReceipt.api -> create_goods_receipt.
- Tao phieu nhap, chi tiet, cong ton, cap nhat cost_price, ghi stock transaction.

20.5 Bao cao doanh thu loi nhuan
- ReportsPage -> report.api.revenue.
- Backend ReportService lay orders completed va order_details.
- Revenue = tong final_amount.
- COGS = sum(quantity * cost_price snapshot).
- Profit = revenue - COGS.

20.6 AI phan tich doanh thu
- ReportsPage bam AI -> report.api.aiAnalysis.
- Backend tong hop metrics that -> prompt -> Groq -> parse JSON -> luu ai_revenue_analyses.

20.7 Offline POS
- Online sync products/categories/customers ve IndexedDB.
- Offline doc local va luu pending order.
- Online lai offlineSync day pending orders len API.
- Server van kiem tra ton kho that trong transaction.

20.8 Scanner offline queue
- POS tao pairing code/channel.
- Scanner quet barcode -> gui barcode_scanned kem scanId.
- POS nhan -> gui barcode_ack.
- Scanner chi xoa queue khi co ACK.
- Neu mat mang/khong ACK -> luu AsyncStorage, online lai retry FIFO.

20.9 PayOS/VietQR
- TransferPaymentModal tao QR.
- Neu PayOS config, backend tao payment link va luu payment_intents.
- Webhook PayOS verify va update status.

21. CAU HOI HOI DONG CO THE HOI VA GOI Y TRA LOI
===============================================================================

CAU 1: De tai nay giai quyet van de gi?
Tra loi:
He thong giai quyet viec ban hang tai quay, quan ly san pham, ton kho, hoa don,
ca lam, doanh thu va phan tich AI cho cua hang ban le. No thay the cach quan ly
thu cong bang Excel va giam sai sot ton kho/doanh thu.

CAU 2: He thong khac CRUD binh thuong o diem nao?
Tra loi:
Ngoai CRUD, he thong co checkout transaction, tru kho atomic, audit log, ca lam,
bao cao loi nhuan bang snapshot gia von, offline POS, scanner realtime/offline queue
va AI phan tich doanh thu.

CAU 3: Vi sao tach frontend, backend, database?
Tra loi:
Frontend chi xu ly giao dien, backend xu ly auth/validation/business logic,
database dam bao rang buoc va transaction. Tach nhu vay de de bao tri, test va mo rong.

CAU 4: Vi sao dung route-controller-service?
Tra loi:
Route khai bao URL/middleware, controller nhan request/response, service chua nghiep vu
va truy van CSDL. Cach nay giup code ro lop, de test va tranh controller qua day.

CAU 5: Vi sao checkout nam trong SQL RPC?
Tra loi:
Checkout gom nhieu buoc phai nhat quan: tao order, tao detail, payment, tru kho,
ghi stock transaction, audit. Dat trong SQL function giup transaction rollback toan bo
neu bat ky buoc nao loi.

CAU 6: Lam sao tranh hai thu ngan ban cung mot san pham lam am kho?
Tra loi:
Trong create_pos_order, database lock dong san pham bang FOR UPDATE. Giao dich thu hai
phai cho giao dich thu nhat xong roi moi doc ton kho moi.

CAU 7: Vi sao khong xoa hoa don khi huy?
Tra loi:
Hoa don la chung tu kinh doanh. Xoa se mat lich su. He thong chuyen status cancelled,
payment refunded, co the hoan kho va ghi audit log.

CAU 8: Gia von trong bao cao lay o dau?
Tra loi:
Lay tu order_details.cost_price duoc snapshot tai thoi diem ban. Nho vay khi gia von
san pham thay doi sau nay, loi nhuan qua khu van dung.

CAU 9: AI doanh thu co lay du lieu that khong?
Tra loi:
Co. Backend tong hop doanh thu, top products, category sales, payment stats va metrics
tu database, sau do dua vao prompt. Ket qua AI duoc luu kem metrics snapshot.

CAU 10: AI co the bia so lieu khong?
Tra loi:
He thong giam rui ro bang cach tinh metrics o backend truoc, prompt yeu cau AI dua vao
so lieu da cung cap va tra JSON. Tuy nhien AI van la external model, nen ket qua duoc
luu lai va co metrics snapshot de doi chieu.

CAU 11: Offline POS hoat dong nhu the nao?
Tra loi:
Khi online, app sync san pham/danh muc/khach hang ve IndexedDB. Khi mat mang, POS doc
du lieu local va luu pending order. Khi online lai, offlineSync gui don len backend.

CAU 12: Neu offline ban hang nhung server da het ton thi sao?
Tra loi:
Khi sync len server, create_pos_order van kiem tra ton kho that trong transaction.
Neu khong du ton, don sync failed va nguoi dung duoc bao loi. Server la nguon dung cuoi.

CAU 13: Scanner realtime hoat dong ra sao?
Tra loi:
POS tao pairing code va Supabase Realtime channel. Scanner quet QR pairing, ket noi cung
channel, quet barcode va gui event cho POS.

CAU 14: Scanner mat mang co mat barcode khong?
Tra loi:
Khong. Scanner co offline queue bang AsyncStorage. Moi scan co scanId, POS nhan se gui ACK.
Neu khong co ACK, scanner giu trong queue va retry khi online.

CAU 15: Bao mat API nhu the nao?
Tra loi:
Backend dung JWT, bcrypt hash password, authMiddleware, roleMiddleware, Zod validation,
rate limit va error handler tap trung. Frontend chi an menu, backend moi la lop bao ve chinh.

CAU 16: Neu user sua localStorage de doi role thi sao?
Tra loi:
Khong duoc, vi backend khong tin localStorage. Backend verify JWT va nap lai user active
tu database, role duoc kiem tra o backend.

CAU 17: JWT luu localStorage co han che gi?
Tra loi:
Phu hop do an/demo, nhung production nen nang cap httpOnly cookie + refresh token de giam
rui ro XSS lay token.

CAU 18: Audit log dung de lam gi?
Tra loi:
Luu vet cac hanh dong quan trong nhu tao/huy don, nhap kho, dieu chinh kho. No giup truy vet
ai lam gi, luc nao, noi dung thay doi la gi.

CAU 19: Role admin/manager/cashier khac nhau the nao?
Tra loi:
Admin co toan quyen, manager quan ly van hanh/bao cao/kho, cashier chu yeu ban hang va ca cua minh.
Backend route se gioi han quyen theo role.

CAU 20: Vi sao dung Supabase/PostgreSQL?
Tra loi:
PostgreSQL manh ve quan he, constraint, index, transaction va JSONB. Supabase cung cap managed DB,
SDK, realtime, service role va de deploy cho do an.

CAU 21: Vi sao dung JSONB?
Tra loi:
Phu hop du lieu linh hoat nhu settings, audit metadata, AI analysis result, metrics snapshot.

CAU 22: Index trong database de lam gi?
Tra loi:
Tang toc truy van theo email, category, created_at, status, audit, payment_intents...

CAU 23: PayOS tich hop nhu the nao?
Tra loi:
Frontend goi backend tao payment link/QR, backend luu payment_intents. Webhook PayOS duoc verify,
sau do update payment_intents de doi soat.

CAU 24: Vi sao can payment_intents?
Tra loi:
De luu trang thai giao dich ngoai he thong: pending, paid, failed, cancelled. Neu webhook cham/loi,
he thong van co ban ghi doi soat.

CAU 25: Ca lam tinh chenh lech tien mat the nao?
Tra loi:
expected_cash = opening_cash + cash_sales + cash_in - cash_out.
cash_difference = closing_cash - expected_cash.

CAU 26: Thong bao ton kho thap tao nhu the nao?
Tra loi:
Sau ban/nhap/dieu chinh, service sync stock alert. Ton <= 0 la out_of_stock, ton <= min_stock_level
la low_stock, nguoc lai resolve.

CAU 27: Neu email/Telegram/AI service bi loi thi he thong co ngung ban hang khong?
Tra loi:
Khong nen. Cac tac vu phu nhu email/notification/AI duoc tach rieng, luong ban hang cot loi van do
transaction database xu ly.

CAU 28: Test da co gi?
Tra loi:
Co backend test cho API docs, cache, SQL enterprise, pos calculations. CI chay backend test,
backend build, frontend build va scanner typecheck.

CAU 29: Huong phat trien tiep theo?
Tra loi:
Them E2E Playwright, Payment Center UI, bao cao loi nhuan nang cao, auth httpOnly cookie,
FIFO/weighted average cost, monitoring/logging, backup, mobile app day du.

CAU 30: File nao quan trong nhat neu hoi code?
Tra loi:
- database/enterprise_pos_core.sql: transaction checkout/huy/nhap.
- backend/src/services/order.service.ts: goi RPC order.
- backend/src/services/report.service.ts: bao cao/AI doanh thu.
- frontend/src/pages/pos/POSPage.tsx va POS hooks: man hinh ban hang.
- frontend/src/services/offlineDB.ts/offlineSync.ts: POS offline.
- sora-scanner/App.tsx/offlineQueue.ts: scanner realtime va offline queue.
